For general questions about the i4MDS Consortium, the DataSpace, or the Data Access Portal, please contact the consortium coordination team.

consortium@i4mds.org

For technical problems related to the portal, dataset links, or access to the Zenodo DataSpace, please contact the portal support team.

support@i4mds.org
