i4MDS DATA GOVERNANCE

Data Access Policy

Principles and conditions governing access to multi-omics datasets generated by the i4MDS Consortium.

Version 1.0 · August 2026
01

Purpose

The i4MDS Data Access Portal provides a centralized framework for requesting access to multi-omics datasets generated within the i4MDS Consortium.

The purpose of this policy is to facilitate responsible scientific use of these datasets while protecting research participants, respecting applicable regulations, and ensuring appropriate recognition of the researchers and institutions that generated the data.

02

Data Access

Datasets available through the i4MDS DataSpace may be subject to different levels of access depending on their scientific, ethical, legal, and governance requirements.

  • Open datasets may be accessed according to the terms specified by the corresponding repository.
  • Restricted datasets require an access request and approval before the data can be released.
  • Additional conditions may apply to specific datasets depending on their source, consent framework, or applicable agreements.
Controlled access

Access to restricted datasets is granted only after evaluation and approval of the proposed research use by the appropriate i4MDS governance body.

03

Application and Review

Researchers requesting access to restricted datasets should submit a research proposal through the Request Access page.

Applications may be evaluated based on the scientific rationale, proposed use of the data, relevance of the requested datasets, qualifications of the research team, and the proposed data management procedures.

The i4MDS Consortium reserves the right to request additional information when necessary to evaluate an application.

04

Permitted Use

Approved data may be used only for the scientific purposes described in the approved application.

Researchers should not use the data for purposes that are materially different from those described in the approved proposal without obtaining appropriate authorization.

Access to the data does not confer ownership of the underlying datasets or biological samples.

05

Data Security and Confidentiality

Researchers receiving access to restricted datasets are responsible for maintaining appropriate security and confidentiality safeguards.

  • Data should be stored using appropriate institutional or otherwise secure infrastructure.
  • Access should be limited to authorized members of the approved research team.
  • Researchers must not attempt to identify or re-identify individual participants.
  • Restricted data must not be redistributed to unauthorized third parties.
  • Researchers should comply with all applicable institutional, ethical, and legal requirements.
06

Data Sharing

Restricted datasets obtained through the i4MDS Data Access Portal must not be shared, transferred, or redistributed to third parties without prior authorization.

Collaboration with additional researchers or institutions that requires access to restricted data may require an additional review or amendment to the approved project.

07

Publications and Scientific Outputs

Researchers using i4MDS datasets are expected to appropriately acknowledge the i4MDS Consortium and cite the relevant dataset and associated publications in scientific outputs resulting from the use of the data.

Where applicable, dataset DOIs and the original publications describing the data should be cited.

Specific authorship, acknowledgement, or publication requirements may apply to individual datasets or collaborative projects.

08

Duration and Termination of Access

Access to restricted datasets is granted for the purposes and duration associated with the approved research project.

The i4MDS Consortium may suspend or terminate access when there is evidence of misuse, violation of the approved research purpose, breach of data security, or non-compliance with applicable requirements.

09

Data Protection and Regulatory Compliance

Researchers are responsible for ensuring that their use of i4MDS datasets complies with applicable data protection legislation, institutional policies, ethical approvals, and other relevant regulatory requirements.

Where datasets contain or derive from human subject data, additional requirements may apply according to the relevant consent, ethical, legal, and governance framework.

10

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as the i4MDS data-sharing framework, governance procedures, or applicable regulations evolve.

The version applicable to a specific data request will be the version in effect at the time of the approval, unless otherwise specified.

Questions about data access?

If you have questions about dataset availability, eligibility, or the access process, please visit the Contact page or submit a data access request .
